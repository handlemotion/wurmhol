---
title: "Glossary"
description: "What we call things, and why."
---

**Wurmhol**, a slightly mispronounced German way to say wormhole, a shortcut from a sandbox to your browser.

**Pipe**, one private link to one port. You open one with `npx wurmhol <port>` and it ends when it's stopped or out of time.

**dirt**, the console at [dirt.wurmhol.com](https://dirt.wurmhol.com), where you sign in, see the wall and manage tokens and members. Worms live in it. `app.wurmhol.com` takes you there too.

**Wall**, dirt's main page, every open pipe in your org around the Create pipe button. Press one to see its link, who started it and when, and to open or end it.

**Org**, who a pipe belongs to and who can open it. You can be in more than one and switch between them in dirt.

**Owner** and **member**, owners invite people with an invite link, remove them, change roles, hand the org over and pay. Members open, see and end pipes.

**Token**, a `wh_…` key that lets a machine or an agent open pipes for an org. Tokens belong to the org, not to a person.

**Named pipe**, a pipe opened with `--name`, so its URL stays the same every time.

**Relay**, the part of Wurmhol in between, it checks who you are and forwards requests to the machine running the dev server. Also the [OAuth relay](/pipes#apps-with-sign-in), which sends sign-in callbacks back to the right pipe.
