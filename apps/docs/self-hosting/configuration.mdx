## Sign-in

Passkeys work with no setup. For Google or GitHub, create OAuth clients with these callbacks and set `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` or `GITHUB_CLIENT_ID`, plus the matching `_SECRET` as a secret.

- Google, `https://dirt.example.com/api/auth/callback/google`
- GitHub, `https://dirt.example.com/api/auth/callback/github`

Magic links go out through Cloudflare Email Service. Onboard your domain under Email Service, Email Sending, and set `MAIL_FROM`, like `sign-in@example.com`. There's no API key, the `EMAIL` binding in `wrangler.jsonc` is the credential. Without `MAIL_FROM`, sign in with OAuth and add a passkey.

## Billing

Leave `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` empty and every org opens pipes without a plan. To charge, set up Stripe like this.

1. One product with a yearly price and, if you want, a monthly one. Their ids go in `STRIPE_PRICE_YEARLY` and `STRIPE_PRICE_MONTHLY`.
2. A webhook to `https://dirt.example.com/api/auth/stripe/webhook` for `checkout.session.completed` and `customer.subscription.created`, `.updated` and `.deleted`, with its signing secret as `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.
3. The customer portal turned on in Stripe's settings.

There's no trial, an org subscribes before its first pipe.

## Limits

| Var                     | Default | What it limits, per org               |
| ----------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------- |
| `LIMIT_ACTIVE_SHARES`   | 5       | Pipes open at once                    |
| `LIMIT_MONTHLY_BYTES`   | 100 GB  | Traffic a month                       |
| `LIMIT_SHARE_SECONDS`   | 12 h    | The longest `--ttl`                   |
| `DEFAULT_SHARE_SECONDS` | 4 h     | A pipe's lifetime when it doesn't ask |

`RATE_LIMIT_AUTH`, `RATE_LIMIT_MAGIC_LINK`, `RATE_LIMIT_CONNECT` and `RATE_LIMIT_MANAGE` take `<count>/<seconds>`, or `off`.

## Docs link

The wall's Docs links go to `https://wurmhol.com/docs`. Set `DOCS_URL` to send them to your own docs instead.

## Cleanup

Once a day the Worker deletes pipe history older than `RETENTION_DAYS` (90), plus expired sessions, sign-in links, invite links and device logins. `wrangler deploy` sets that up. It only writes its own log lines, Cloudflare's invocation logs are off so request URLs aren't recorded. Every var is in [`apps/worker/.dev.vars.example`](https://github.com/handlemotion/wurmhol/blob/main/apps/worker/.dev.vars.example).
