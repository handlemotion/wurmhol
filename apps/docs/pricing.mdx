---
title: "Pricing"
description: "Personal at $12 a year, Team at $15 a month, per org, each with a 14-day trial."
---

Wurmhol has two plans, priced per org. Personal is $12 a year for you and up to two others. Team is $15 a month, or $150 a year, for any number of people. Viewers are members too, so on Team every client or teammate you share with is included.

Each org's first plan starts with a 14-day trial. Checkout takes a card, and the plan is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel first. An owner starts the trial from the wall, and pipes start right after. Switching plans, moving Team to yearly and cancelling are all under Billing on the wall.

## Limits

| Per org            | Personal      | Team           |
| ------------------ | ------------- | -------------- |
| Members            | 3             | Any number     |
| Pipes open at once | 3             | 15             |
| Traffic            | 25 GB a month | 250 GB a month |
| Viewer requests    | 5 M a month   | 50 M a month   |
| Longest pipe       | 12 hours      | 12 hours       |

These are fair-use limits. Traffic and requests reset on the 1st of each month (UTC). If you need more, or you'd rather not pay at all, you can [run your own](/self-hosting), the code is open source.
