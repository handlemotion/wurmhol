---
title: "Pricing"
description: "$12 a year per org."
---

Wurmhol is $12 a year per org, however many people are in it. We went yearly because card fees eat most of any small monthly price, and per org because what costs us money is traffic, not seats.

There's no trial or free tier yet. An owner subscribes from the wall before the org's first pipe, and pipes start right after.

## Limits

| Per org            | Limit          |
| ------------------ | -------------- |
| Pipes open at once | 5              |
| Traffic            | 100 GB a month |
| Longest pipe       | 12 hours       |

These are fair-use limits. If you need more, or you'd rather not pay at all, you can [run your own](/self-hosting), the code is open source.
