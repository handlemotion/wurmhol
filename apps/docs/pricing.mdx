---
title: "Pricing"
description: "Friends at $15 a year, Business at $20 a month plus requests, per org, each with a 14-day trial."
---

Wurmhol has two plans, priced per org. You pay for requests, the one thing that costs us money: pipes sitting open, members and traffic are generous on both.

**Friends** is $15 a year, for people sharing work with people. Up to 10 members, 25 pipes open at once and 2 million viewer requests a month, plenty for a handful of agents running in parallel.

**Business** is $20 a month with 10 million requests included, then $1.50 per million, for developers building Wurmhol into their own tools. Any number of members, up to 500 pipes at once. A spend cap, $100 a month unless an owner changes it under Org settings, keeps a runaway agent from running up a bill: at the cap, pipes stop until the next billing period or a higher cap.

Each org's first plan starts with a 14-day trial. Checkout takes a card, and the plan is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel first. Business usage during the trial is free and stops at its included 10 million requests. An owner starts the trial from the wall, and pipes start right after. The card, invoices and cancelling are under Billing on the wall. To move between plans, cancel (the unused time is credited) and start the other.

## Limits

| Per org            | Friends        | Business                        |
| ------------------ | -------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Viewer requests    | 2 M a month    | 10 M included, then $1.50 per M |
| Pipes open at once | 25             | 500                             |
| Members            | 10             | Any number                      |
| Traffic            | 100 GB a month | 10 TB a month                   |
| Longest pipe       | 12 hours       | 12 hours                        |

Friends counts by calendar month (UTC), Business by its billing period. If you need more, or you'd rather not pay at all, you can [run your own](/self-hosting), the code is open source.
