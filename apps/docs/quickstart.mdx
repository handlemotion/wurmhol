---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Share a dev server in two commands."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get a token">
    Sign in at [dirt.wurmhol.com](https://dirt.wurmhol.com), subscribe your org, and create a
    token (menu → Tokens). On your own computer, `npx wurmhol login` does this for you: it prints
    a code to approve in the browser and stores the token in `~/.wurmhol/credentials.json`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a pipe">
    ```bash
    npx wurmhol 8787
    ```

    It prints `https://<id>.wurmhol.dev` and returns. The URL is new every time.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it">
    Tap the link on your phone or laptop and sign in once. Every open pipe is also on your org's
    wall, by page title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop it">
    ```bash
    npx wurmhol stop 8787
    ```

    A pipe also ends when anyone in the org ends it from the wall, or after 4 hours.

  </Step>
</Steps>

Hot reload works: browser WebSockets pass through the pipe, and the CLI sends
`Host: localhost:<port>`, so Vite and most dev servers need no config.
